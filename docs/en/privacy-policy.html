Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. UnderPressure has been designed as a personal wellness journal where all data is stored exclusively on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and what rights you have.

1. Data Collection

UnderPressure collects no personal data automatically.

All information is manually entered by you. The app does not access sensors, contacts, location data, or other system data. You have complete control over what you record.

You can manually enter the following types of data:

  • Daily readings and observations
  • Personal notes and reflections
  • Custom measurements and trackers
  • Wellness check-ins
  • Any personal information you choose to log

2. Data Storage

All data is stored exclusively on your device.

The app:

  • sends no data to servers
  • synchronizes no data with cloud services
  • uses no external databases
  • shares no data with third parties

You maintain full control over your data at all times.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

User data is stored on your device and remains there until you manually delete it.

You can remove your data in the following ways:

  • Delete All Data from the App: Use the app's settings to remove all your recorded entries and notes.
  • Clear Application Data (Android): Go to device Settings → Apps → UnderPressure → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all data stored by the app.
  • Uninstall the Application (Android): Long-press the UnderPressure app icon on your home screen or app drawer, select "Uninstall", and confirm. You can also uninstall through device Settings → Apps → UnderPressure → Uninstall. This removes the app and all associated data from your device.

4. Data Sharing

The app includes optional sharing and export functions.

Data is only shared or exported if you explicitly choose to do so. The app never automatically uploads or sends data anywhere.

5. No Third-Party Services

UnderPressure uses no:

  • Analytics tools
  • Ad networks
  • Tracking libraries
  • Third-party SDKs
  • External links

There are no ads, no payments, and no subscriptions.

6. Security

Since all data remains on your device, its security depends on:

  • Your device's operating system
  • Your device's lock screen and security settings

The app does not transmit or expose your data externally.

7. Information for Users

UnderPressure provides informational references about daily rhythm, balance states, activity levels, recovery patterns, and personal wellness routines. These are educational references only and do not constitute medical advice or professional guidance.

8. Children's Privacy

UnderPressure is not directed at children and collects no data automatically. All data is manually entered by the user and is unpersonalized.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
