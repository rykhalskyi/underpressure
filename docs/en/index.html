UnderPressure

Your Personal Blood Pressure Tracking App

Welcome

UnderPressure is a simple and privacy-focused mobile application for tracking your blood pressure and heart rate measurements on your Android device.

All your data stays on your device – we don't collect, store, or share any of your health information with third parties.

Features

✓ Local data storage – all measurements remain on your device
✓ Easy-to-use interface for recording blood pressure and pulse
✓ No account required
✓ No ads or trackers
✓ Share your data only when you choose to
